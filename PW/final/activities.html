Activities

Jump to: Water · Forest · Art · Evenings

Water

Every morning the children learn to swim or improve their style in the shallow part of the lake. Older groups go canoeing on Tuesday and Thursday, always with a life jacket and two instructors.

Back to top

Forest

On Wednesday we hike to the lake on the other side of the hill. On Friday the treasure hunt takes the whole morning. Good shoes, a hat and a water bottle are needed every day.

Back to top

Art

Painting workshop, crafts with natural materials, and rehearsals for the Friday theatre show. The paintings go home with the children.

Back to top

Evenings

Board games, a quiz night and, on Thursday, the barbecue at the campfire. Lights out at 21:30.
